# GHOSTPROTOCOL SRSL-1.1 prospective migration — 2026-10-17

Current first-party GHOSTPROTOCOL materials are offered under [SRSL-1.1](LICENSE). The original SRSL-1.0 text is archived at [LICENSES/SRSL-1.0.txt](LICENSES/SRSL-1.0.txt). Prior valid rights in copies distributed under old licences are not withdrawn retroactively. Third-party code remains subject to its own licences.

## Historical packaging inconsistency

Earlier `pyproject.toml` and `setup.py` advertised MIT licensing, while the root `LICENSE` claimed SRSL-1.0. These inconsistent representations are now aligned to SRSL-1.1 **prospectively**. This correction does not purport to revoke rights a recipient may have validly acquired or relied on under previous terms. Any dispute over those earlier grants requires qualified legal review.

The 50% profit-participation proposal remains negotiable rather than an automatic debt. The AI-training restriction has statutory exceptions. No activation gate, DRM or executable logic is added in this change.
